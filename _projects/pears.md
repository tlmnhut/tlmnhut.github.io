summary: >-
  A decentralized search engine designed to run locally on your machine, with full control
  over privacy.
---

[PeARS](https://pearsproject.org/) is a search engine that does not need a data center. Each
instance indexes a slice of the web locally, and instances share their indexes rather than their
users' queries — so searching does not mean handing your history to anyone.

I was a member of the project, which is open source and community-run.
